Luter

AI-Powered Learning Assistant

Our Mission

Luter transforms the way students learn by leveraging artificial intelligence to create personalized, efficient, and engaging educational experiences. We believe that every student deserves access to intelligent tools that adapt to their unique learning style.

Brand Values

  • Intelligence: Smart AI that understands and adapts
  • Accessibility: Learning tools for everyone, everywhere
  • Innovation: Cutting-edge technology meets education
  • Growth: Empowering students to reach their full potential

Logo Usage Guidelines

  • Minimum clear space: 1x the logo height
  • Minimum size: 24px for digital, 0.5" for print
  • Use on light backgrounds with primary purple
  • Inverted version available for dark backgrounds
